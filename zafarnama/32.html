कौशलस्य पतिः स वै निर्बलानां स पालकः ।
दीनबन्धुः सतां चैव खलानां कूलसूदनः ॥ ३२ ॥
Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
कौशलस्य कौशलस्य
Of cleverness / prudence / skill

Grammar Masculine/Neuter, Genitive, Plural (Base 'निर्बल')

He

Of the wicked / evil ones
Grammar Masculine, Genitive, Plural (Base 'खल')
कूलसूदनः कूल-सूदनः
Destroyer of the lineage/family (of the wicked)
Grammar Masculine, Nominative, Singular
Samasa कूलं (वंशं) सूदयति (नाशयति) इति कूल-सूदनः (उपपद-तत्पुरुषः)
Translation
Hindi: वह चतुरता (बुद्धिमत्ता) का स्वामी है, निर्बलों का रक्षक (पालक) है। वह दीनों का सच्चा मित्र (दीनबन्धु) है, सज्जनों का सहायक है और दुष्टों के सम्पूर्ण वंश का विनाश करने वाला है।

English: He is the Lord of prudence (wisdom) and the Escort (protector) of the weak. He is the Saviour of the poor (friend of the distressed), the helper of the righteous, and the destroyer of the lineage of the wicked.
