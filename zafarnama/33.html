धर्मस्य पालकः स हि गुरुताभिः परिप्लुतः ।
सत्यस्य सवै ज्ञातास्ति श्रुतीनां सवै कारकः ॥ ३३ ॥
Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
धर्मस्य धर्मस्य
Of righteousness / faith / divine law
Grammar Masculine, Genitive, Singular (Base 'धर्म')
पालकः पालकः
The protector / saviour
Grammar Masculine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'पालक')
स हि सः हि
He indeed
Grammar सः: Pronoun (Base 'तद्'), Masculine, Nominative, Singular
हि: Indeclinable (Emphasis particle)
गुरुताभिः गुरुताभिः
With greatness / eminences
Grammar Feminine, Instrumental, Plural (Base 'गुरुता')
परिप्लुतः परिप्लुतः
Immersed / filled / overflowing
Grammar Past Passive Participle (Kta) of root 'प्लु' with prefix 'परि', Masculine, Nominative, Singular
सत्यस्य सत्यस्य
Of truth / facts
Grammar Neuter, Genitive, Singular (Base 'सत्य')
सवै स वै
That very (Lord) indeed
Grammar सा: Feminine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'तद्')
एव: Indeclinable (Emphasis)
ज्ञातास्ति ज्ञाता अस्ति
Is the knower / aware
Grammar ज्ञाता: Masculine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'ज्ञातृ', root 'ज्ञा')
अस्ति: Root 'अस्', Lat Lakara (Present Tense), 3rd Person, Singular
श्रुतीनां श्रुतीनाम्
Of the scriptures / Vedas
Grammar Feminine, Genitive, Plural (Base 'श्रुति')
कारकः कारकः
The creator / inspirer / author
Grammar Masculine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'कारक', root 'कृ')
Translation
Hindi: वह (परमात्मा) धर्म (सदाचार व नियमों) का रक्षक (पालक) है और बड़प्पन (महानता) से ओत-प्रोत है। वह परम सत्य का ज्ञाता है और श्रुतियों (पवित्र धर्मग्रंथों) का रचयिता व प्रेरक है।

English: He is the Saviour of Faith (Dharma) and overflowing with eminence (greatness). He is the Knower of truth (facts) and the Inspirer/Creator of scriptures.
