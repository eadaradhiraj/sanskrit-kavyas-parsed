कण्ठग्रहं न वात्येव भर्तुः क्रुद्धाऽपि यत्नतः ।
कङ्कणश्रेणिकेवासौ दोषमेवावलम्बते ॥ २३६०
Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
कण्ठग्रहं कण्ठ-ग्रहम्
Embrace around the neck (holding the neck)
Grammar Masculine, Accusative, Singular (Base 'कण्ठग्रह')
Samasa कण्ठस्य ग्रहः (आलिङ्गनम्) इति कण्ठ-ग्रहः (षष्ठी-तत्पुरुषः), तम् ।
न वात्येव न वाति एव
Does not give up indeed / does not leave
Grammar न: Negative particle
वाति: Root 'वा' (to move away / leave / blow), Lat Lakara, 3rd Person, Singular
एव: Indeclinable (Emphasis)
भर्तुः भर्तुः
Of the husband
Grammar Masculine, Genitive, Singular (Base 'भर्तृ')
क्रुद्धाऽपि क्रुद्धा अपि
Even though angry / offended
Grammar क्रुद्धा: Feminine, Nominative, Singular (Kta-pratyaya of root 'क्रुध्')
अपि: Indeclinable (Concession)
यत्नतः यत्नतः
Deliberately / with effort
Grammar Adverbial form using -tasi (तः) suffix from base 'यत्न'
कङ्कणश्रेणिकेवासौ कङ्कण-श्रेणिका इव असौ
Like a series/row of bangles, she
Grammar कङ्कण-श्रेणिका: Feminine, Nominative, Singular
इव: Comparison particle
असौ: Pronoun (अदस्), Feminine, Nominative, Singular (She/Heroine)
Samasa कङ्कणानां श्रेणिका इति कङ्कण-श्रेणिका (षष्ठी-तत्पुरुषः) ।
दोषमेवावलम्बते दोषम् एव अवलम्बते
Clings only to the arm / holds onto the fault (double entendre / Shlesha)
Grammar दोषम्: Double meaning (श्लेष):
1. दोस् / दोषन् (Arm / Forearm) $\rightarrow$ Accusative Singular.
2. दोष (Fault / Offense) $\rightarrow$ Accusative Singular.
एव: Indeclinable
अवलम्बते: Root 'लम्ब्' with prefix 'अव', Atmanepada, Lat Lakara (Present Tense), 3rd Person, Singular
Translation

English: Even though offended and angry, she deliberately does not release the embrace around her husband's neck. Like a row of bangles, she clings solely to the Dosha (with a pun meaning both: she rests on his arms, and she continually brings up his faults).

Hindi: वह नायिका रूठी और कुपित होने पर भी जान-बूझकर पति के गले का आलिंगन नहीं छोड़ती है। कंगन की पंक्ति के समान वह केवल 'दोष' का ही आश्रय लेती है (यहाँ 'दोष' शब्द में श्लेष अलंकार है—कंगन जैसे पति की भुजाओं पर टिके रहते हैं, वैसे ही नायिका भी पति के दोषों/अपराधों को ही पकड़ कर रखती है)।
