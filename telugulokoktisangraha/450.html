आदौ तातो वरं पश्येत्ततो वित्तं ततः कुलम् ।
यदि कश्चिद्वरे दोषः किं धनेन कुलेन किम् ॥ ४५०
Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
आदौ आदौ
First of all / in the beginning
Grammar Masculine, Locative, Singular (Base 'आदि', used adverbially)
तातो तातः
Father
Grammar Masculine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'तात')
वरं वरम्
The bridegroom / groom
Grammar Masculine, Accusative, Singular (Base 'वर')

Should examine, thereafter
Grammar पश्येत्: Root 'दृश्' (पश्), Vidhilin Lakara (Potential Mood), Parasmaipada, 3rd Person, Singular
ततः: Indeclinable (अव्ययम्)
वित्तं वित्तम्
Wealth / financial status
Grammar Neuter, Accusative, Singular (Base 'वित्त')

Then / thereafter
Grammar Indeclinable (अव्ययम्)
कुलम् कुलम्

Grammar Neuter, Accusative, Singular (Base 'कुल')
यदि यदि
If
Grammar Indeclinable (अव्ययम्)
कश्चिद्वरे कश्चित् वरे
Any (fault), in the groom
Grammar कश्चित्: Pronoun, Masculine, Nominative, Singular
वरे: Masculine, Locative, Singular (Base 'वर')
दोषः दोषः
Fault / defect
Grammar Masculine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'दोष')
किं धनेन किम् धनेन
Of what use is wealth?
Grammar किम्: Interrogative pronoun, Neuter, Nominative, Singular
धनेन: Neuter, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'धन')
कुलेन किम् कुलेन किम्
Of what use is noble lineage?
Grammar कुलेन: Neuter, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'कुल')
किम्: Interrogative pronoun, Neuter, Nominative, Singular
Translation
Hindi: पिता को कन्या के विवाह हेतु सबसे पहले वर (उसकी योग्यता व चरित्र) को देखना चाहिए, उसके बाद धन (सम्पत्ति) को, और फिर कुल (खानदान) को देखना चाहिए। यदि वर में ही कोई गंभीर दोष या अवगुण है, तो अकूत धन से क्या लाभ और उच्च कुल से क्या प्रयोजन?

English: A father should first examine the groom himself (his virtues and character), then his wealth, and then his family lineage. If there is some serious defect in the groom himself, of what use is wealth, and of what use is noble lineage?
