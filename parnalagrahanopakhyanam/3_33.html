Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
ततस्तेनातिशब्देन ततः तेन अति-शब्देन
Then by that deafening blast of sound
Grammar Instrumental Sg.
व्याप्तमासीद्दिशान्तरम् व्याप्तम् आसीत् दिग्-अन्तरम्
The horizons all around were filled
Grammar व्याप्तम्, आसीत्: Neuter Nom. Sg.
दिग्-अन्तरम्: Neuter Nom. Sg.
अध्यक्षोऽभूद्यामिकानां अध्यक्षः अभूत् यामिकानाम्
There was the captain of the night-guards
Grammar अध्यक्षः: Masc. Nom. Sg. (Captain)
यामिकानाम्: Masc. Genitive Plural (Of watchmen)
बाबूखानेति नामकः बाबूखानः इति नामकः
Named Babu Khan
Grammar बाबूखानः, नामकः: Masc. Nom. Sg.
Translation

English: That deafening roar echoed across the entire horizon. The captain of the fort's night-watch was an officer named Babu Khan...
