Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
झटित्यन्यविमार्गेण झटिति अन्य-विमार्गेण
Instantly via another secret back-path
Grammar झटिति: Adverb (Instantly)
अन्य-विमार्गेण: Masc. Instrumental Sg. (विमार्ग = गुप्त/उल्टा मार्ग)
जीवमात्रेण निर्गतः जीव-मात्रेण निर्गतः
Escaped with bare life
Grammar जीव-मात्रेण: Instrumental Sg.
निर्गतः: Past Participle (Masc. Nom. Sg.)
तत्पश्चान्मौक्तिकाख्योऽसौ तत्-पश्चात् मौक्तिक-आख्यः असौ
After that, this Motyaji Ravlekar
Grammar मौक्तिक-आख्यः, असौ: Masc. Nom. Sg. (Motyaji)
तृतीयस्तत्पलायनम् तृतीयः तत्-पलायनम्
The third hero, his flight
Grammar तृतीयः: Masc. Nom. Sg.
तत्-पलायनम्: Neuter Accusative Sg.
Translation

English: Nagoji Pandit immediately fled through a secret back-route with his bare life. Thereafter, the third Maratha commander Motyaji Ravlekar, observing his flight...
