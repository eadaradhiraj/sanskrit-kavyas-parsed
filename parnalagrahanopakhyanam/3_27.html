Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
आनाजिपण्डितैः आनाजि-पण्डितैः
By Annaji Datto Pandit
Grammar Masculine, Instrumental, Plural (Honorific)
तेषां पश्चाद्भागे तेषाम् पश्चात्-भागे
In the rear of those commandos
Grammar तेषाम्: Genitive Plural
पश्चात्-भागे: Locative Sg.
महावने निलीय महा-वने निलीय
Concealing himself in the dense forest
Grammar महा-वने: Neuter Locative Sg.
निलीय: Gerund (Lyap) of 'नि-ली'
सेनया सार्धं पृष्ठरक्षतया स्थितम् सेनया सार्धम् पृष्ठ-रक्षतया स्थितम्
Remained as rearguard reserve along with troops
Grammar सेनया: Fem. Instrumental Sg.
पृष्ठ-रक्षतया: Fem. Instrumental Sg. (As rearguard cover)
स्थितम्: Impersonal Past Participle (भावे क्त)
Translation

English: Annaji Datto Pandit, concealing himself in the dense jungle behind the scaling party with his supporting troops, remained stationed to provide rear protection.

Hindi: आणाजी पंडित ने चढ़ाई करने वाले वीरों के पीछे घने जंगल में अपनी सेना के साथ छुपकर पृष्ठ-रक्षक (रिज़र्व फ़ोर्स) के रूप में मोर्चा संभाला।
