Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
शार्दूलनखवत् शार्दूल-नखवत्
Curved like tiger-claws
Grammar Adverbial / modifier (-vati)
लोहनिर्मितैर्नखरैर्युतान् लोह-निर्मितैः नखरैः युतान्
Equipped with steel climbing-hooks / claws
Grammar Masc. Accusative Plural (qualifying soldiers)
गोधाधरान् कीलधरान् गोधा-धरान् कील-धरान्
Holding monitor-lizards (ghorpad) and iron pitons/spikes
Grammar Masculine, Accusative, Plural
Samasa गोधां (घोरपड़) धरन्ति इति गोधा-धराः (उपपद) । कीलान् धरन्ति इति कील-धराः (उपपद) ।
वलीमुखशिलीमुखान् वलीमुख-शिलीमुखान्
Agile like monkeys and piercing like arrows
Grammar Masc. Accusative Plural (Adjective to soldiers)
Translation

English: Equipped with tiger-claw-like steel climbing hooks (Wagh-nakh), carrying monitor lizards (Ghorpad) and iron spikes, agile like monkeys and lethal like arrows...

Hindi: बाघ के नाखूनों जैसे लोहे के अंकुश (वाघ-नख) पहने, घोरपड़ (गोधा) और लोहे की कीलों को धारण किए हुए, वानरों के समान फुर्तीले और बाणों के समान अचूक उन सैनिकों को...
