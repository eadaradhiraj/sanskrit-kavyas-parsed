Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
अथ स्तुते बन्दिभिः अथ स्तुते बन्दिभिः
Now, while being praised by bards (Sati-saptami)
Grammar स्तुते: Past Participle, Locative Sg.
बन्दिभिः: Instrumental Plural
अन्वयज्ञैः सोमार्कवंश्ये अन्वय-ज्ञैः सोम-अर्क-वंश्ये
Knowing pedigrees; belonging to Solar and Lunar lines
Grammar अन्वय-ज्ञैः: Instrumental Plural (अन्वयं जानन्तीति - उपपद)
सोम-अर्क-वंश्ये: Locative Sg.
नरदेवलोके नर-देव-लोके
In the assembly of mortal kings
Grammar Locative Singular
Samasa नराणां देवाः नरदेवाः (राजानः) । तेषां लोकः (समूहः), तस्मिन् ।
सञ्चारिते चागुरुसारयोनौ धूपे सञ्चारिते च अगुरु-सार-योनौ धूपे
And when fragrant aloe incense was wafted about
Grammar सञ्चारिते, धूपे: Locative Sg.
अगुरु-सार-योनौ: Locative Sg. (अगरु का सार है कारण जिसका)
समुत्सर्पति वैजयन्तीः समुत्सर्पति वैजयन्तीः
Rising higher above the fluttering banner flags
Grammar समुत्सर्पति: Present Participle (Shatr), Locative Sg.
वैजयन्तीः: Fem. Accusative Plural (Flags/banners)
Translation

English: Now, while the entire assembly of kings of the Solar and Lunar dynasties was being eulogized by genealogical bards, and while the fragrant smoke of black aloe incense curled upward, overtopping the decorative banners...

(Verses 8, 9, and 10 form a syntactic Visheshaka completing with Indumati's entry in verse 10).

Hindi: जब वंशावली के ज्ञाता भाटों द्वारा सूर्य और चंद्र वंश के राजाओं की स्तुति गाई जा रही थी, तथा काले अगरु की सुगन्धित धूप का धुआँ उत्सव-पताकाओं को पार करता हुआ ऊपर आकाश में उठ रहा था...
