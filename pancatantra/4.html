Translation & Context

Prose Narrative:
The King continued: "Therefore, some practical means must be devised immediately by which their intellects may be awakened."
Thereupon, the scholars spoke up one by one: "O Majesty! Grammar alone takes full twelve years to study. If that is somehow mastered, only then can treatises on ethics and polity be grasped; and only after that does true intellectual awakening dawn."
Then from among them, an enlightened minister named Sumati spoke: "Your Majesty, human life is fleeting and uncertain, whereas the linguistic sciences take ages to master! Therefore, let some condensed essence be devised for the rapid education of these princes. For it is said —"

Verse 4 Translation:
English: "The science of language is indeed shoreless and without end; human life is exceedingly brief, and the obstacles in it are manifold. Therefore, grasping only that which is the distilled essence is to be pursued—just as swans extract pure milk from the midst of water."
