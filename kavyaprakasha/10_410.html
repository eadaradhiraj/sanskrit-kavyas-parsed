अनयेनैव राज्यश्रीर्दैन्येनेव मनस्विता ।
मम्लौ साऽथ विषादेन पद्मिनीव हिमाम्भसा ॥ १०-४१०
Padaccheda & Samasa Analysis
अनयेनैव अनयेन एव
By bad policy / lack of good governance, indeed
Grammar अनयेन: Masculine, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'अनय')
एव: Indeclinable (अव्ययम्)
Samasa न नयः इति अनयः (नञ्-तत्पुरुषः), तेन ।
राज्यश्रीर्दैन्येनेव राज्य-श्रीः दैन्येन इव
Royal splendour; by wretchedness, as if
Grammar राज्य-श्रीः: Feminine, Nominative, Singular
दैन्येन: Neuter, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'दैन्य')
इव: Indeclinable
Samasa राज्यस्य श्रीः इति राज्य-श्रीः (षष्ठी-तत्पुरुषः) ।
मनस्विता मनस्विता
High-mindedness / magnanimity
Grammar Feminine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'मनस्विता')
मम्लौ मम्लौ
Withered / faded / grew dejected
Grammar Root 'म्लै' (to wither/fade), Lit Lakara (Perfect Tense), Parasmaipada, 3rd Person, Singular
साऽथ सा अथ
She (the heroine), then
Grammar सा: Feminine, Nominative, Singular (Pronoun 'तद्')
अथ: Indeclinable (Adverb - then)
विषादेन विषादेन
Through sorrow / depression
Grammar Masculine, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'विषाद')
पद्मिनीव पद्मिनी इव
A lotus plant, as if
Grammar पद्मिनी: Feminine, Nominative, Singular (Base 'पद्मिनी')
इव: Indeclinable (Comparison particle)
हिमाम्भसा हिम-अम्भसा
By frost / icy water
Grammar Neuter, Instrumental, Singular (Base 'हिमाम्भस्')
Samasa हिमं च तत् अम्भश्च इति हिम-अम्भः (कर्मधारयः), तेन ।
Translation

English: She withered through depression just as royal splendour fades through bad policy, as high-mindedness is ruined through wretchedness, or as a lotus plant withers through frost.

Hindi: जैसे कुनीति (नीति के अभाव) से राज्य की लक्ष्मी क्षीण हो जाती है, दीनता से उदात्त-मनस्कता मलीन हो जाती है, और पाला (बर्फ़ीले जल) पड़ने से कमलिनी मुरझा जाती है; वैसे ही वह (नायिका) दुःख और विषाद के कारण सर्वथा मुरझा गई।
